# FIDELITY BUSINESS GROUP
## Premium Corporate Website — Master Development Prompt

### 1. YOUR ROLE

Act as a world-class digital product designer, award-winning creative director, senior frontend engineer, 3D web experience designer, UX strategist, brand identity specialist, and conversion optimization expert.

Your task is to design and develop a premium, visually distinctive, responsive, production-ready website for Fidelity Business Group, an Ethiopian multidisciplinary business group headquartered in Wolaita Sodo, Ethiopia.

Do not create a generic corporate template, a basic business landing page, or a website that simply displays text and cards.

Create an immersive digital experience that communicates architectural excellence, engineering expertise, craftsmanship, creativity, reliability, and business ambition.

The final website should feel comparable in quality to the digital experiences of leading international architecture firms, luxury interior design studios, and premium multidisciplinary design companies.

The design should be ambitious enough to compete for international web design recognition while remaining practical, accessible, fast, and commercially effective.

**Primary creative concept: FROM THOUGHT TO EMPIRE.**

Use the company's existing slogan as a central part of its brand story.

The website must help Fidelity Business Group:
- Establish a strong and credible digital presence.
- Present its different business divisions under one cohesive corporate identity.
- Showcase architectural, engineering, furniture, interior design, and printing projects.
- Attract individual clients, property developers, investors, businesses, institutions, and potential partners.
- Generate qualified project inquiries and sales leads.
- Build trust through real projects, professional presentation, and clear communication.
- Strengthen its position in Wolaita Sodo and expand its visibility throughout Ethiopia.

### 13. 3D, MOTION, AND INTERACTION DESIGN

Make the website feel modern and immersive without turning it into a technology demonstration.

Use motion to support storytelling, establish hierarchy, and improve the perception of quality.

#### Recommended techniques

1. Scroll-triggered reveals for headings and imagery.
2. Subtle parallax on large architectural photographs.
3. Image clipping and masking transitions.
4. Smooth page transitions where practical.
5. Sophisticated project-card hover interactions.
6. Subtle 3D tilt on selected interactive elements.
7. Animated geometric shapes inspired by the company symbol.
8. Refined loading and transition states.
9. Interactive project filtering.
10. A restrained animated brand introduction.
11. Carefully designed cursor interactions on desktop, where useful.
12. A sophisticated footer reveal.
13. Optional 3D architectural model interaction.
14. Subtle depth and perspective effects on selected compositions.

#### 3D implementation

Evaluate Three.js or React Three Fiber for genuinely useful 3D experiences.

Use CSS transforms and perspective effects for simpler interactions.

Use a motion library such as GSAP or Framer Motion where appropriate to the selected stack.

Do not add every library simply because it is available.

Choose the smallest reliable set of tools that produces the desired experience.

#### Motion rules

- Respect the user's reduced-motion preference.
- Avoid scroll hijacking.
- Avoid forced horizontal scrolling.
- Keep navigation responsive during animation.
- Avoid excessive spinning, bouncing, or glowing.
- Ensure that animations do not block content or forms.
- Use lightweight alternatives on mobile.
- Avoid unnecessary WebGL rendering.
- Ensure all critical content remains accessible if animations fail.

The result should feel cinematic, deliberate, and professional.

### 18. PERFORMANCE AND ACCESSIBILITY

A beautiful website must also be fast, accessible, and technically sound.

#### Performance requirements

- Use responsive images.
- Prefer modern image formats such as WebP or AVIF where supported.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery.
- Avoid unnecessarily large video files.
- Compress and optimize 3D assets.
- Load expensive visual effects only when necessary.
- Minimize unnecessary JavaScript.
- Avoid layout shifts.
- Reserve image dimensions.
- Optimize font loading.
- Use appropriate caching.
- Keep the initial page load lightweight.
- Test on slower mobile connections.

Aim for strong Core Web Vitals and a Lighthouse score of at least 90 in Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO where practical.

Treat these scores as targets, not guarantees. Measure the actual deployed website and address bottlenecks rather than manipulating test results.

#### Accessibility requirements

- Use semantic HTML.
- Provide keyboard navigation.
- Ensure visible focus states.
- Use sufficient color contrast.
- Provide meaningful image alt text.
- Associate labels with form fields.
- Support screen readers.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid communicating essential information through color alone.
- Ensure interactive elements have accessible names.
